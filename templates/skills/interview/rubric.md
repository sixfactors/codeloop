# Interview rubric

What the interview stage must satisfy before it counts as done. Attest lines go under `## Rubric` at the end of interview.md, after the last question.

I1 | Three to five questions, each with a recommended answer | check
I2 | Each question is tagged scope, acceptance, risk or boundary; at most one other | check
I3 | No question restates something research settled (cite the research line it builds on) | review
I4 | Every R7 open question is asked here or marked settled with a reason | attest
I5 | The recommended answer is the one an owner in a hurry can accept without reading research | review
I6 | Once answered, each answer is folded into spec.md and the field named | attest

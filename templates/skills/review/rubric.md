# Review rubric

What the review stage must satisfy before it counts as done. Attest lines go under `## Rubric` at the end of review.md.

W1 | verify.md says pass before review starts | check
W2 | One live-walk step repeated by the reviewer | attest
W3 | Every finding has a verdict: fix now, defer, unnecessary, with evidence | check
W4 | Every fix-now is fixed and verify re-run | check
W5 | Every defer exists as a proposed card | check
W6 | The verdict line is accept only when W4 holds | check

# Expected grading — with-research

research.md names two external sources and one internal decision. A brief that skips naming any
of them when it talks about the replay window or the compare function has invented the mitigation
instead of citing where it came from — that is what the fourth checklist line is for.

Checklist lines that must be yes:
- brief.md has a Summary section
- the Summary restates the spec's goal, not just its title
- brief.md has a Risks section naming one concrete risk
- when research.md names a competitor or decision, the brief cites it by name

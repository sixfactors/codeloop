## Q1 Scope: should a pack require at least two skills, or is a single-skill pack allowed?
recommended: Require at least two; a single skill already has its own install page, so a one-skill pack would just duplicate it.
answer: Agreed, require at least two skills in a pack.

## Q2 Boundary: does publishing a pack also need version history and deprecation in this epic, or can that wait?
recommended: Wait. Ship publish and install first; versioning is its own story later.
answer: Wait, versioning is out of scope for this epic.

## Q3 Acceptance: who can publish a pack, any workspace member or only an admin?
recommended: Any workspace member who can already edit the skill library; publishing a pack doesn't need a new permission.
answer: Same as skill-library edit permission, no new role.

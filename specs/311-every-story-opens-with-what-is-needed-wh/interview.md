## Q2 Can you edit the summary yourself, and if you do, may the agent change it afterwards?
recommended: Yes, you can edit it. The agent may add to it at the end of its turn but never replaces a line you wrote, and the page shows who wrote each line and when.
answer: 

## Q3 What should the top of a story show before any agent has written its summary?
recommended: A summary worked out from what the board already knows (which step it is on, what is done, whether anything waits on you), marked as not yet written by an agent; the next agent turn replaces it.
answer: 

## Q4 How do we stop a summary going out of date and saying something no longer true?
recommended: Show who wrote it and at which step; once the story has moved past that step, mark it "written before the latest step" until the next agent rewrites it. Anything waiting on you is always taken from the board, never from the text.
